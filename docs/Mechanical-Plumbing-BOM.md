```text

               ↓
        ┌───────────────┐
        │ LARGE HEATSINK│
        └───────┬───────┘
                │
        ┌───────┴───────┐
        │   TEC MODULE  │
        ├───────────────┤
        │ WATER BLOCK   │
        └───────┬───────┘
                │
           WATER LOOP
```

The hot side requires aggressive heat rejection.

The water side should use a proper water block with adequate flow.

# 20. Important Mechanical Requirements

### Tank

The tank and stand should safely support at least:

**227 L water + tank + equipment + substrate**

A practical design load of approximately **350 kg or greater** is recommended.

### Plumbing

All plumbing in contact with water should be:

- Aquarium-safe
- Food-safe where practical
- Corrosion-resistant
- Easy to disassemble
- Leak tested before biological use

### Crayfish Protection

All intakes must have guards.

Crayfish must not be able to:

- Enter pump intakes
- Enter filter plumbing
- Reach moving pump components
- Reach electrical wiring
- Climb into the refill reservoir

### Peltier

The Peltier cooling assembly should be treated as a separate mechanical subsystem.

The hot side must have sufficient thermal rejection capacity.

Do not install the TEC assembly permanently until thermal testing confirms that it can maintain the required water temperature under Philippine ambient conditions.

---

# 21. Mechanical Testing Before Biological Use

Perform the following tests:

- 24-hour leak test
- Pump continuous operation test
- Flow verification
- Drain test
- Refill test
- Solenoid leak test
- High-level overflow test
- Low-level protection test
- Filter blockage test
- Air pump continuous operation test
- Peltier water-block leak test
- Peltier condensation test
- Peltier thermal test
- 72-hour continuous circulation test
- Preferred 7-day system reliability test

**Do not introduce crayfish until the complete mechanical, plumbing, electrical, and thermal systems have passed testing.**
